How the TTO functions

The ORSP compliance contact page names a Research Compliance Coordinator/Specialist (IRB/IACUC-focused) but no technology transfer or licensing officer -- inquiries route straight to Tiwari. He also chairs the CSU systemwide Council of Chief Research Officers, a cross-campus role that further competes for his bandwidth. Essentially not a distinct function -- a compliance-adjacent duty on a very busy AVP's plate.

Likely pain points & why

No dedicated tech-transfer staff at all
With disclosures, patenting, and licensing routed through the AVP personally, triage speed and follow-through on faculty invention disclosures are almost certainly slow and inconsistent -- there's no one whose full-time job is moving disclosures through the pipeline.
Rising research volume with flat TT capacity
CSUF maintained its R2 designation in 2025 (min. $5M R&D spend, 20+ research doctorates/year) and FY24-25 grants/contracts hit $35M, the third-highest total in university history -- more federally-funded research typically means more disclosable IP, without any scaled-up TT capacity to match.
Tiwari himself is stretched thin
Full engineering faculty load (250+ published articles, ongoing mentorship of 240+ students), a systemwide CSU chair role, plus running the AVP office -- bandwidth, not motivation, is likely the real constraint.
