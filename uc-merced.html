How the TTO functions

Per UC Merced's Oct 2024 org chart, Yeung holds Senior IP Officer and is the senior-most staffed IP position in OTIIR -- the AVC for OTIIR seat, an Industry Alliances Specialist role, a Lab Assistant role, and an IP Officer 1 role are all listed vacant. Only other named staff: Kira Bustillos (IP Officer 1), Margie Loza (Project Policy Analyst), Carla LaBass (Executive Assistant). OTIIR reports toward the Vice Chancellor for Research and runs licensing, industry sponsored research/NDAs, a tech-discovery portal (IN-PART), and entrepreneur programming (NSF I-Corps, a Venture Lab).

Likely pain points & why

R1 status just achieved, right as disclosure volume is spiking
UC Merced achieved R1 classification in Feb 2025 -- explicitly about sustained, high research output. Disclosures grew from 8 (FY2023) to 20 (FY2024), a 150% jump.
Commercialization output hit a wall exactly as disclosures spiked
In the same FY23-24 window, utility licenses issued fell to zero (from 1), startups formed fell to zero (from 1), and licensing income dropped to about $2,000 (from $6,000). That's about as clean a “rapidly growing pipeline hitting a wall at the commercialization stage” signal as exists -- and it lines up exactly with the vacant AVC seat and vacant staff positions.
Skeleton-crew office doing R1-caliber commercialization work
With the AVC seat and multiple staff roles vacant, Yeung is likely the most senior IP person actually processing this growing pipeline -- a genuine capacity mismatch, not a general guess.
