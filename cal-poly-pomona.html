How the TTO functions

Tech transfer is one duty layered onto the AVP for Research and Innovation role, alongside the Office of Research & Sponsored Programs (proposal/award administration, ~5 staff) and the Office of Research Compliance (IRB/animal research). There's minimal dedicated tech-transfer staff beyond LaMunyon and admin support -- no visible licensing-officer-level staff dedicated to deal execution. Entrepreneurship/incubation (“CPP Invests,” the Innovation Orchard) is organizationally split off under a different AVP entirely.

Likely pain points & why

One person wearing both the “grow research” and “protect/monetize IP” hats
LaMunyon likely has limited time/resources to proactively manage a licensing pipeline, disclosure triage, or industry-facing relationships the way a dedicated TTO director would -- a structural, not personal, constraint.
Just went permanent in the role
LaMunyon became permanent AVP in July 2024 after 3 years as interim -- still establishing a permanent-role agenda, likely without bandwidth to build out a true licensing function on top of everything else.
Confirmed, direct federal funding disruption
An April 2025 Poly Post article reports three suspended USDA grants, a terminated faculty research grant despite prior approval, and diminished NSF fellowship opportunities -- against a typical CPP baseline of $25-35M/year in federal research funding, with LaMunyon referenced directly on CPP's funding baseline.
