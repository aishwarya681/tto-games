How the TTO functions

The Office of Innovation & Corporate Partnerships sits within the Division of Research and Innovation. Confirmed team: Andrew Exner (also Executive Director of the SJSU Research Foundation), Jason Kim (Manager, Research Corporate Partnerships), Sandeep Mukkamala (IP Specialist), Jalyn Pambid (Innovation Programs Facilitator), plus the closely linked SpartUp Incubator team. The office publishes actual “Featured Opportunities” one-pagers for specific inventions (a modular exoskeleton, a neural-network in-memory computing patent, a smart breast pump, a boronated nanoscale substrate) -- a real, marketed patent portfolio, not just a policy page.

Likely pain points & why

Leadership interim at multiple levels simultaneously
Marc d'Alarcao served as Interim VP for Research and Innovation for two years; SJSU just announced Crist S. Khachikian will become the permanent VP starting July 27, 2026. Meanwhile Exner's own Office of Innovation directorship is also explicitly “Interim.” Stacked interim leadership typically means new priorities and reporting relationships, and likely some pause on major strategic commitments until Khachikian settles in.
Rapid research growth without confirmed added TTO scaling
SJSU achieved its first-ever R2 Carnegie designation in 2025, based on $83.4M in FY2023 research expenditures -- a 16% YoY increase. Interim VP d'Alarcao stated SJSU is “far above the R2 threshold” and is actively pursuing R1 status. Growing research volume without confirmed added licensing/IP staff is a real, evidence-grounded pressure point.
Growing but still being cut
SJSU has one of the few growing CSU enrollments (4% jump, largest freshman class in history) yet still faces additional budget cuts beyond $58M already cut over the prior two years, with vacant positions going unfilled and 8%-per-department cuts -- an unusual “growing but still being cut” dynamic.
