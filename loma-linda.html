Loma Linda University

Zoran Zivanovic — Director, Industrial Relations (Research Affairs)
The most entrepreneurial/spinout-oriented office of the 17 -- runs n3EIGHT, a dedicated business incubator (est. 2016) that houses startups, not just a licensing desk. LLU Health received a record $85M in grants in FY2023.

How the TTO functions

Industrial Relations is the tech transfer arm of Research Affairs, led by VP Michael Samardzija (a biotech attorney turned researcher). Zivanovic brought 30+ years of startup experience to the Director role. n3EIGHT, LLU's incubator, houses portfolio companies (Step Lite, iEye, StemRex, Tack, and others) at a dedicated off-campus facility -- one portfolio company, Aditxt, went public in July 2020. This is a hybrid TTO-plus-incubator model, operationally heavier than pure licensing.

LLU Health received $85M in grants in FY2023 -- its highest ever, with VP Samardzija noting the rate of award per submission continues to climb. Rising grant volume typically means rising invention-disclosure volume feeding into Industrial Relations.

Because n3EIGHT actually houses and supports startups (capital, lab space, infrastructure) rather than just executing license agreements, Zivanovic likely juggles landlord/operator-style logistics alongside classic IP/licensing administration -- a distinct, probably under-discussed pain point.
Small office, no published staff directory
No public data suggests Industrial Relations has more than a handful of staff -- the office gives one general contact line rather than a team directory, suggesting Zivanovic runs day-to-day disclosure/licensing/incubator logistics without heavy staff support.
