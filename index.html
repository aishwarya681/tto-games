California Universities — TTO Rundown

17 university named accounts in CA — pain points, TTO structure, recent news, contacts, and suggested outreach for each.
Verify before reaching out — 6 flagged accounts
  • University of California, Davis: Office was just reorganized (Oct 2025) -- Tudor is ~10 months into the role and industry contracting was spun off into a new unit (RAISA).
  • University of California, Merced: The AVC for OTIIR position is vacant -- Yeung is functionally the senior-most staffed IP role in the office right now.
  • University of California, Santa Cruz: IMPORTANT: Pesce has left UCSC for UC Agriculture & Natural Resources (UC ANR). The current UCSC licensing/industry-alliances lead is most likely Jeffrey Matthew Jackson (Director, Innovation Transfer) -- verify before calling.
  • California State Polytechnic University, Pomona: No dedicated TTO -- tech transfer is folded entirely into LaMunyon's AVP role, separate from the “CPP Invests”/Innovation Orchard incubator run by a different AVP (Dr. Olukemi Sawyerr).
  • California State University, Los Angeles: Ye recently replaced longtime AVP Jeffrey Underwood (7 years in the role, departed for UNC Greensboro) -- even the CSU systemwide directory still lists Underwood, so treat that reference data as stale.
  • California State University, Sacramento: UNCERTAIN: OITT's own current staff page does not list Gacel-Sinclair, and shows the Technology Transfer Officer role as vacant. His current employment status could not be confirmed -- verify gently at the start of any call (“wanted to reach out to whoever's leading tech transfer these days”) rather than assuming he's still there.
  • Claremont Graduate University: CGU has run operating deficits for 10+ years and is in active merger talks -- Pomona College was reported (Dec 2025) as the lead acquisition candidate. Approach this account with real sensitivity given the institutional stakes.
  • San Francisco State University: SF State announced plans (Feb 2025) to close its Romberg Tiburon marine research campus over a ~$4M deferred-maintenance bill -- a stark, on-record signal of research-vs-teaching budget tension at the leadership level.
  • San Jose State University: Leadership is interim at multiple levels -- Exner's own directorship is Interim, and SJSU just announced Crist S. Khachikian as the new permanent VP for Research and Innovation, starting July 27, 2026.
