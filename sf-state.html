How the TTO functions

ORSP (Office of Research and Sponsored Programs) has roughly 20 professional staff plus 12 student assistants, organized around grants administration, compliance, IRB/IACUC, and HR for grant-funded staff -- not a dedicated licensing/commercialization unit. The Intellectual Property page is largely definitional (what is tech transfer, disclosure form) with no data on deal volume or dedicated licensing staff. This is one of the least commercialization-forward offices of the 17.

Likely pain points & why

Likely a relatively new leadership seat
Comparing SF State's 2020 org chart (which shows Michael Scott as AVP) to the current Provost's Direct Reports page shows Scott has since moved to Vice Provost for Academic Resources and Pelton now holds the AVP for Research and Sponsored Programs seat -- exact transition date unconfirmed, but Pelton is likely relatively new to this specific leadership seat, which typically brings onboarding-era pain points.
Severe, ongoing CSU-wide and SF State-specific budget crisis
SF State enrollment fell 26% between 2015 and 2024; the CSU system faces an ~8% state funding cut (~$375M systemwide); President Lynn Mahoney has publicly discussed cutting athletic programs, consolidating low-enrollment majors, and exploring shared administrative services with neighboring CSUs.
A direct, named threat to a specific research facility
SF State announced in Feb 2025 plans to close the Romberg Tiburon marine research campus (53 acres) due to a ~$4M deferred-maintenance bill, despite active federally-relevant research. Provost Amy Sueyoshi was quoted directly: “it's actually not OK for us to have so much of our resources flowing in a direction that doesn't serve our undergraduate students directly” -- a stark signal of research-vs-teaching tension.
Two-front budget squeeze
President Mahoney has specifically flagged that federal funding cuts are threatening the College of Science and Engineering, which she called “spectacularly successful” under prior federal grant regimes -- meaning Pelton's office may be facing shrinking federal award pipelines on top of state cuts.
