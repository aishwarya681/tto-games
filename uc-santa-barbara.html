Named Account · California

University of California, Santa Barbara

Claire Driscoll — Director, Office of Technology & Industry Alliances (TIA)
TIA is a mature, ~9-person office with full delegated authority to negotiate both licensing agreements AND industry research agreements -- one of the first TTOs structured this way. New VP for Research (Rachel Segalman) started July 2025.

How the TTO functions

Established in 2005, TIA sits under the Vice Chancellor for Research. Team: Director (Driscoll), Associate Director of Industry Contracts (Kevin Stewart), two Senior Licensing Officers, a Licensing Analyst, two Industry Contracts Officers, and a Marketing Manager. A mature, licensing-heavy office with real industry-alliance muscle, not a bare-bones function.

Likely pain points & why

Federal funding whiplash is the dominant pressure right now
UCSB town halls (Aug 2025-mid 2026) describe an estimated $12B in NIH cuts nationally, $783M in NIH grant terminations tied to DEI-related programs, and direct project terminations at UCSB (glioblastoma treatment research, Europa-habitability laser instrumentation, cancer/Alzheimer's projects). This squeezes the pipeline of fundable, disclosure-worthy research that eventually reaches TIA.
Leadership transition at the top of the research hierarchy
VP for Research Joe Incandela (TIA's boss, who hired Driscoll in 2023) completed 8 years and was replaced by Rachel Segalman effective July 1, 2025. New leadership plus budget pressure is a classic moment where TTOs get asked to justify headcount/ROI or absorb new strategic priorities.
Rising commercialization demand without a parallel funding cushion
UCSB's CNSI technology incubator just marked 10 years and 40 startups (June 2025); the New Venture Competition is in its 26th year with strong throughput -- more inventions moving toward commercialization even as federal grant terminations threaten the front end of the pipeline.

In the news / recent involvement

March 2024 -- Ingantec Corporation signed an exclusive patent license for III-nitride/MOCVD microLED display technology; Driscoll quoted directly in the release.source ↗
June 2025 -- CNSI's on-campus technology incubator celebrated its 10-year anniversary: 40 startups spun out.
Feb 2025 -- RazeMath (AI math tutoring) won UCSB's 26th annual New Venture Competition.
