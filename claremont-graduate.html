How the TTO functions

ORSPG sits under Academic Affairs (not a VP for Research), led by Associate Provost Eusebio Alvaro with Rusli as Director, plus a Certified IRB Professional and a Senior Grants and Contracts Analyst -- 4 people total. Function is entirely pre-award/post-award grants administration and compliance; there is no commercialization, licensing, or IP marketing function described anywhere. This is the most “not a distinct TTO function” of the 17 schools researched.

Likely pain points & why

CGU is in serious, publicly-reported financial distress and is being acquired
CGU has run operating deficits for over 10 years; retained Tyton Partners in July 2024 to find a merger/partnership (100+ potential partners contacted by Jan 2025); as of Dec 2025, Pomona College is reported as the lead acquisition candidate. CGU officials stated: “we do not have the financial resources to continue going it alone as a graduate-only, comprehensive university,” with a projected ~$8.7M operating loss for FY2026.
Grants/research funding named as one of the only realistic survival levers
Then-Interim President Tim Kirley explicitly said the fix must include “diversified revenue sources including philanthropy, research grants, and endowment growth” -- meaning Rusli's grants-administration function is one of the levers leadership is counting on, raising stakes and workload on a very small team amid real institutional uncertainty.
Leadership instability
CGU has cycled through multiple interim presidents during this period -- a genuinely turbulent leadership environment for anyone on staff, let alone someone trying to grow grant revenue.
