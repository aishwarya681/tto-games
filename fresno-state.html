How the TTO functions

Confirmed via the office's own staff page: a Director, 3 Contracts and Grants Officers, a Research Compliance Officer, a Research Operations Analyst, admin support, and 2 pre-award coordinators -- zero staff carry a licensing/IP-specific title. There's an invention disclosure form and an IP/patent policy page, but nothing resembling a licensing-officer function.

Likely pain points & why

Record research volume colliding with an active hiring freeze
Research funding has broken its own record for six consecutive years ($54.7M → $70.7M → $78.3M, FY2022-24) while, in spring 2025, the university faced a $19M budget deficit and implemented a hiring freeze for non-essential positions. A grants office processing record award volume with a frozen ability to add headcount is a textbook overload scenario.
New AVP still settling in
Bush served as Interim AVP from January 2025 and was only confirmed permanent in April 2026 -- just a few months into the permanent seat, on top of running a pure grants-administration function with an under-built IP/commercialization capability.
No IP/licensing capacity to build on
With zero dedicated licensing staff, inventors wanting to disclose/patent/license something are routed through generalist grants staff already stretched by record award volume -- a plausible source of slow turnaround on invention disclosures.
