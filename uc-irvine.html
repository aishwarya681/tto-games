University of California, Irvine

Ronnie Hanecak, Ph.D. — Senior Director of Licensing, UCI Beall Applied Innovation
UCI Beall Applied Innovation, led by Chief Innovation Officer Errol Arkilic. FY2024: startups formed nearly tripled (3 to 10) while licensing income fell sharply ($4.4M to $1.8M).

How the TTO functions

Beall Applied Innovation has four units: Research Translation Group (patent/licensing -- Hanecak's Invention Transfer Group), Enterprise Collaborations Group (industry sponsored research), New Ventures Group (entrepreneurship, I-Corps, Proof of Product funding), and The Cove @ UCI (a 31,000 sq ft startup facility). ~40 staff total. Under Arkilic (since Aug 2021), the office has visibly leaned into spinout/venture creation as its public identity.

Likely pain points & why

Real, quantified federal funding damage
UCI's own Research Town Hall materials (May 2025) report 56 grant awards affected (5 suspended, 51 terminated), $36.5M at risk, and total awards down from $670M to $558M year over year, with proposed FY2026 cuts of 37% (NIH) and 56% (NSF) under discussion. This directly pressures Beall to demonstrate non-federal revenue value at a moment federal funding is shrinking.
Startup formation surging while licensing income cratered
FY2023 to FY2024: startups formed nearly tripled (3 to 10) -- a genuine success story -- but total licensing income fell from $4.434M to $1.8M. A believable signature of a licensing team stretched thin supporting a fast-growing venture pipeline while legacy licensing deals aren't being replaced at the same rate.
Leadership framing that raises translation-volume pressure
Chief Innovation Officer Errol Arkilic has publicly framed the university's stature as “a function of its impact on society... turning research into products and services” -- signaling leadership measures the office on translation volume, raising pressure on Hanecak's Research Translation Group to keep pace.
