University of California, Berkeley

Laleh Shayesteh — Director, IP and Administration, Office of Technology Licensing (IPIRA)
IPIRA / Office of Technology Licensing (OTL) -- one of the highest-throughput, most mature TTOs in the country. FY2024: 174 disclosures, 267 patent applications filed, 73 issued, 20 licenses, $28.9M licensing income (up from $12.5M in FY2023).

How the TTO functions

IPIRA (Intellectual Property & Industry Research Alliances) sits under Assistant Vice Chancellor Carol Mimura and splits into OTL (patenting/licensing, ~11 staff, where Shayesteh sits) and the Industry Alliances Office (~7 staff, 1,300+ sponsored-research/industry contracts), plus a small Equity Solutions unit. This is a diversified, high-volume operation -- 310+ startups launched historically, $31.3B+ VC raised, $250M+ cumulative licensing revenue -- not a scrappy under-resourced shop.

Likely pain points & why

Lean staff relative to volume, with active open positions
The March 2025 IPIRA org chart shows two unfilled roles (a Licensing Officer and a Senior Industry Liaison/Contracts Officer) even as FY2024 volume (174 disclosures, 267 applications filed) is handled by ~11 OTL staff -- a concrete, current understaffing signal on top of already-high per-officer caseloads.
Federal funding volatility pushing more weight onto licensing
UC's president called potential federal research cuts “catastrophic” (Feb 2025); Berkeley lost ~$3.5M in federal funding for language/international institutes; as recently as May 2026 the Trump administration suspended Berkeley grants over foreign-funding allegations researchers dispute. This pushes faculty and the university toward licensing/industry revenue as an alternative, raising pressure on OTL to move faster with the same headcount.
Rising revenue expectations
Licensing income more than doubled year over year ($12.5M to $28.9M FY23-24) -- the office is clearly being counted on as a growing revenue line, raising the stakes on throughput and triage quality.

In the news / recent involvement

Steady stream of spinout wins in 2026: Scribe Therapeutics ($25M+ CIRM funding), a biotech IPO raising $129M, Ambi Robotics/Pickle Robot physical-AI logistics launch, nEye.ai $80M Series C, BreezeBio Series B, multiple FDA IND clearances.source ↗
