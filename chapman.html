Chapman University

Lawrence Lau — Director of Industry Alliances and Commercialization
A genuine one-person office -- Lau is the only named staff member. Chapman reconfirmed R2 Carnegie status in April 2025; commercialization isn't named as a strategic priority in the university's own 2023-2028 plan.

How the TTO functions

Industry Alliances and Commercialization sits under Chapman's Office of Research; the office's own contact page lists only Lau, with corporate donation/gift inquiries explicitly routed elsewhere. An 8-stage disclosure-to-licensing pipeline exists on paper, but there's no public data on deal volume, patent counts, or startups. Office hours are held once a month (3rd Thursday, 3-4pm) -- a small operational detail suggesting a low-bandwidth, appointment-driven practice.

Likely pain points & why

Single point of failure / bandwidth
One director covering disclosure intake, IP committee evaluation, outside-counsel coordination, marketing, and licensing negotiation for the whole university -- backlog and slow cycle times on invention evaluation are a near-certain consequence of the confirmed one-person staffing.
Growing research volume without added TTO capacity
Chapman reconfirmed R2 Carnegie Classification (High Research Spending and Doctorate Production) in April 2025 -- growing doctoral/research output typically increases invention disclosure volume, with no evidence the commercialization office grew alongside it.
Strategic invisibility
The university's own leadership strategic plan doesn't name commercialization/industry partnerships as one of its five priorities -- Lau may be under-resourced and under-championed relative to peer offices, and might welcome a partner who treats his function as important when the institution hasn't prioritized it publicly.
