Named Account · California

California State University, Long Beach

Jade Sche — Director, Innovation & Economic Development
Sche is the ONLY staff member in CSULB's Innovation & Economic Development division -- a genuine one-person tech-transfer shop. CSULB just earned dual R2 + Opportunity Colleges & Universities Carnegie status (Feb 2025, one of only 27 US institutions) while cutting $28M (~5%) from its FY2025-26 budget with a hiring freeze in effect.

How the TTO functions

Tech transfer sits inside the ~40-person Office of Research and Economic Development (ORED), led by AVP Dr. Simon Kim. Within ORED, Innovation & Economic Development is a distinct division led solely by Sche -- confirmed via both the ORED team page and the Jan 2025 staff directory. She's the point of contact for the Disclosure of Discovery or Invention (DDI) form, working alongside a faculty/provost-appointed Advisory Committee on Patents and Copyrights, with peripheral support from the separate Institute for Innovation & Entrepreneurship (IIE, runs the Sunstone Innovation Challenge).

Likely pain points & why

Single point of failure
One director covering all invention disclosures, IP evaluation, and licensing for a newly R2 research university is a capacity mismatch by design -- any volume increase in disclosures has nowhere to go but onto her desk.
Newly dual-designated R2 + Opportunity Colleges & Universities campus (Feb 2025)
One of only 27 institutions nationwide to hold both simultaneously -- a point of institutional pride that almost certainly raises expectations for research/IP output right as the office is a one-person team.
Real, current budget pressure with a hiring freeze in effect
CSULB is cutting $28M (~5% of its $581M operating budget) for FY2025-26 due to an 8% state funding cut, with CFO Scott Apel noting services “will be delayed as we deal with staffing shortages.” Adding headcount to Sche's team is very unlikely in this climate.

In the news / recent involvement

Feb 2025 -- CSULB earned the dual R2 + Opportunity Colleges and Universities Carnegie designation, one of only 27 US institutions to hold both.
The Sunstone Innovation Challenge continues to run annually with $25K+ awarded to student teams; CSULB and the Long Beach Accelerator received $175K combined in grants from Sunstone Management.
Oct 2025 -- CSULB awarded a $2.6M federal engineering grant.
