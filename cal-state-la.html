How the TTO functions

ORSCA (Office of Research, Scholarship and Creative Activities) is fundamentally a funding/proposal-support and compliance office; its own page says it “will assist” with IP/tech transfer but the full team roster includes no technology-transfer or IP-specific staff member at all. Essentially not a distinct function -- whatever IP handling happens appears to be an ad hoc AVP-level responsibility on top of grants/compliance administration.
